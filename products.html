Under the sink

Reverse osmosis for your drinking water.

Two options — the difference comes down to what the extra stage does, not just the number on the box. We'll walk you through which one actually fits your water when we're on site.

6

6-Stage RO System

Sediment filter → carbon → 2nd carbon → RO membrane → post-carbon polish → remineralization or UV.

Removes dirt, chlorine, bad taste, and a large share of dissolved contaminants — salts, metals, and more — through the membrane stage.

UV variant available — better suited to well water. We'll confirm on-site.
7

7-Stage RO System

Everything in the 6-stage system, plus an alkaline/remineralization stage — or a UV sterilizer, depending on your water.

The RO membrane still does the heavy lifting, reducing up to 95–99% of contaminants including lead, arsenic, nitrates and fluoride.

A 7th stage isn't automatically "better" — it's better for a specific reason. Ask us which one applies to you.
