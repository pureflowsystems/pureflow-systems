Water Softeners

Removes hardness (calcium & magnesium) at the point where water enters your home — protecting pipes, water heater, dishwasher and washer for the long run.

Point of entry

One system, every tap in the house.

Installed at your main water line, a softener treats everything downstream — showers, laundry, dishwasher, and drinking water alike.

What it does
  • Removes hardness (calcium & magnesium) at the main line
  • Protects pipes, water heater, dishwasher, washer
  • Automatic backwash & regeneration, digital display
  • Reduces limescale buildup and soap/detergent waste
Built to last
  • Premium ion exchange resin
  • Corrosion-resistant tank construction
  • Simple installation, minimal day-to-day maintenance
  • Compatible with both city and well water
Sizing

Sized to your household, not guessed at.

Grain capacity determines how much hardness your softener can remove before it needs to regenerate. Bigger households and harder water need a bigger tank.

30,000 Grain

Best fit for smaller households — 1 to 3 people, 1 to 2 bathrooms. Handles moderate hardness without oversizing the tank.

45,000 Grain Most Common

Our most installed size — fits typical family homes, 3 to 5 people, 2 to 4 bathrooms, moderate to high hardness.

60,000 Grain

Built for larger households, 5+ bathrooms, high hardness or well water, and homes with heavier day-to-day water use.

Every quote is put together in person, based on your household size and hardness level. Book a free water test and we'll recommend the right size, face to face.
