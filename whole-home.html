Whole home filtration

Whole Home Filtration

Installed at your main line, treats every tap in the house — reducing chlorine, sediment, VOCs and odour without stripping the healthy minerals from your water.

Point of entry

Cleaner water at every tap, not just one.

Unlike a countertop or under-sink filter, whole-home filtration treats the water before it reaches any fixture in the house — and removes far more than just chlorine.

What it removes
  • Chlorine, chloramines & THMs
  • Ammonia, benzene & chromium
  • PCBs, ethylene & other industrial chemicals
  • Dirt and sediment, for cleaner water throughout
Built to last
  • Sediment filtration built in alongside chemical removal
  • Filtered water delivered to every tap in the house
  • Reduces airborne VOCs, improving indoor air quality
  • For municipal water supplies
Two options

Automatic backwash, or zero electricity — your choice.

Both remove the same chemicals and sediment. The difference is how they operate day to day.

Filtermax Premium 10-Year Warranty

Fully automatic backwashing system — no cartridges to replace. Runs on low-voltage 12VAC power, using less than $2 a year in electricity.

Filtermax Superior 5-Year Warranty

A flow-through design that needs no electricity at all — zero cost to operate, with the same chemical and sediment removal as the Premium.
