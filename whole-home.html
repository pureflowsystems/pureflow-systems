Whole home filtration

Whole Home Filtration

Installed at your main line, treats every tap in the house — reducing chlorine, sediment, VOCs and odour without stripping the healthy minerals from your water.

Point of entry

Cleaner water at every tap, not just one.

Unlike a countertop or under-sink filter, whole-home filtration treats the water before it reaches any fixture in the house.

What it does
  • Removes up to 99% chlorine, sediment, and odour
  • Reduces VOCs and other common contaminants
  • Keeps healthy minerals — does not strip TDS
  • Minimal impact on water pressure or flow rate
Built to last
  • High-quality carbon block filtration media
  • Simple DIY-friendly installation and maintenance
  • Individually wrapped cartridges for freshness
  • Compatible with both city and well water
Sizing

Two housing sizes, same coverage.

Both sizes filter every tap in the house — the difference is how much filter media is inside, which affects capacity and how often cartridges need changing.

10" Blue 3/4"

A compact housing that fits tighter spaces — a solid standard-capacity option for smaller households or as a supplemental filter stage.

20" Blue 3/4" Higher Capacity

Roughly double the filter media of the 10" housing — better suited to larger households or heavier usage, with fewer cartridge changes needed.

Every quote is put together in person, based on your household size and water usage. Book a free water test and we'll recommend the right size, face to face.
